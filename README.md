# 🍎 2026–2027 All-in-One Digital Teacher Planner & Gradebook
> **Modern Pro Educator Operating System** — Interactive Offline Web Application + 35-Page Hyperlinked GoodNotes iPad PDF.

A comprehensive classroom management and lesson planning application designed for K-12 educators, professors, homeschoolers, and tutors. Built with a modern, high-contrast SaaS interface, zero subscription fees, and 100% offline private local storage.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Educator Hubs

### 1. 📊 Teacher Dashboard
* At-a-glance overview of enrolled students, active class rosters, and overall grade averages.
* Quick-action buttons to plan lessons, record grades, pick random students, and print sub folders.

### 2. 🗓️ Advanced Scheduling Engine
* **Rotating Day Cycles (Day 1 – Day 6/8):** Automatically accounts for school rotations without falling behind.
* **Alternating Weeks (Week A / Week B):** Automatic support for alternating morning/afternoon schedules.
* **1-Click Lesson Bumping:** Shift planned lessons forward by 1 or more days after unexpected snow days or school assemblies.

### 3. 📖 Interactive Lesson Planner
* Comprehensive lesson cards with learning objectives, state standards (NGSS/Common Core), timed activity flows (Warm-Up, Direct Instruction, Guided Practice, Exit Ticket), materials checklists, and homework logs.
* 1-Click duplicate and bump tools.

### 4. 📝 Weighted Digital Gradebook
* Category-weighted scoring (Homework, Labs, Quizzes, Unit Exams) with automated grade calculations.
* **Formula Transparency Modal:** Click any student's average to view the exact mathematical formula, category weights, and point contribution breakdown.
* Automatic letter grade scale mapping (A+, A, B, etc.).

### 5. 📍 Classroom Attendance Tracker
* Daily and monthly attendance logs with Present (P), Absent (A), Tardy (T), and Excused (E) status codes.
* 1-Click **"Mark All Present"** and **"Copy to Other Classes"** to eliminate repetitive roll-calling.

### 6. 🪑 Interactive Seating Chart & Randomizer
* 3x4 visual classroom desk grid with front-of-room whiteboard orientation.
* 1-Click **"Shuffle & Randomize"** to create fresh student pairings.
* A–Z sorting and visual accommodation tags (IEP/504).

### 7. 🧰 Live In-Class Teacher Toolkit
* **Random Student Name Picker:** Animated spinner wheel with celebration toast.
* **Smart Student Grouping Generator:** Automatically divides your class into balanced pairs, trios, or groups of 4.
* **Classroom Noise Level Visualizer:** Sound level bar to help students self-monitor volume during independent study or group discussions.
* **Countdown Focus Timers:** Quick presets (2m drill, 5m warmup, 15m lab, 25m focus sprint) with visual alert chime.
* **House / Team Points Scoreboard:** Live counter for classroom gamification.

### 8. 📑 Emergency Substitute Teacher Plan
* 1-Click print-ready substitute folder formatted for standard US Letter / A4 paper.
* Includes bell times, classroom routines, emergency phone extensions, reliable student helpers, and today's lesson plan.

### 9. 📦 Supplies Inventory & Budget Tracker
* Track classroom inventory with low-stock alerts.
* Classroom spending log with reimbursement status.

### 10. 🔒 Privacy & 100% Offline Persistence
* Complies with student data privacy guidelines — data is saved directly in browser `localStorage` and never sent to external servers.
* **1-Click JSON Backup & Restore:** Easily export backups or transfer your planner between school computers.
* Switch between **Pre-Loaded Demo Data** (to explore) and a **Clean Blank Planner** (ready for your own school).

## 🚀 Quick Start (Zero Setup Required)

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser:
```bash
# On Mac:
open index.html

# On Windows:
start index.html

# On Linux:
xdg-open index.html
```

---

## 📱 iPad / Tablet Home Screen App (Optional)

1. Open `index.html` in Safari on your iPad.
2. Tap the **Share** button (box with upward arrow).
3. Select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. The planner now launches as a standalone, full-screen tablet app!
